Over the past four years, my focus has grown from foundational algorithmic theory into building production-ready AI systems and scalable web platforms.

My engineering approach is anchored in solving tangible real-world problems. I built AgriPulse (AgriMitra 360)—a multimodal smart agriculture platform integrating PyTorch leaf disease detection, OpenCV HSV lesion severity calculations, ReAct autonomous agents with tool calling, and bilingual Malayalam/English voice interaction.
